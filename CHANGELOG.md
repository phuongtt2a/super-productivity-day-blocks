# Release notes

## 1.0.0 — Your day, one block at a time

**The first stable Day Blocks release**, accepted after real-app testing.

- **See your whole day.** Plan tasks and habits across Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night in one left-to-right timeline, with Unassigned alongside it on desktop.
- **Plan the way you work.** Drag items into blocks, arrange their order, set exact start/end times or flexible durations, and continue the same task or habit in more than one block without duplication.
- **Build repeatable routines.** Assign habitual times to scheduled habit days while keeping your actual progress separate from planned time.
- **Keep tasks within reach.** Click task and subtask titles to edit their original details in Super Productivity. The timeline stays intact when the app's details panel opens.
- **Make every session clear.** Soft, independent session backgrounds; quick icon controls for progress and ordering; convenient Add session and Remove buttons; centered color popup.
- **Apply colors and continue.** Preset palettes or custom sliders and hex colors. Applying a custom color saves it and immediately closes the popup.
- **Work comfortably anywhere.** Theme and host font compatibility, wallpaper-friendly transparency, keyboard-accessible controls, and a continuous horizontal timeline on small screens.
- **Keep your existing workflow.** Native task and habit progress are not copied. Day Blocks uses its own planning preferences and does not change Today+.

**Getting started:** Download `day-blocks-super-productivity.zip` from the [official v1.0.0 release](https://github.com/phuongtt2a/super-productivity-day-blocks/releases/tag/v1.0.0) and install it through Super Productivity's plugin settings.

## 0.1.1-beta.6 — Apply color and close

- Selecting **Apply color** now saves the custom session color and closes the popup immediately.
- The color stays saved when reopened. Invalid hex codes keep **Apply color** unavailable instead of closing.
- Preset colors can still be previewed without automatically dismissing the popup.
- Preserve the fixed four-block timeline when editing tasks and the inline custom-color controls.

## 0.1.1-beta.5 — Keep the daily timeline intact while editing tasks

- Keep Unassigned beside the timeline when Super Productivity opens its task details and narrows the available space.
- Preserve Morning → Afternoon → Evening → Night in one horizontal row; scroll sideways rather than collapsing the columns or enlarging each card.
- Replace the native custom-color chooser with simple in-popup color sliders and a hex field. Custom colors can be applied and the popup closed normally, avoiding a stuck system picker.
- Keep the compact phone layout and all existing scheduling and task-detail actions unchanged.

## 0.1.1-beta.4 — Open original task details

- Click or tap task titles directly from Unassigned or any planned session to open the task's original details in Super Productivity.
- Click subtask titles to open the subtask's own details; complete checkboxes and drag handles remain independent.
- Keep habits, planned time, existing session colors, four-column order and clean popup behavior unchanged.

## 0.1.1-beta.3 — Centered color popup and faster session actions

- Open the session More menu as a centered popup, rather than beside a card.
- Place **Add session** and **Remove** side by side on each card, directly under the five-icon toolbar. The popup now focuses on choosing a background color.
- Keep the popup open while the board refreshes automatically; stop dismissing it simply because task or habit data updates.
- Keep the original timeline, drag actions, existing session colors and slate-gray Night styling.

## 0.1.1-beta.2 — One-line controls and a quiet daily timeline

- Display five icon-only session actions on one row: Done or habit progress, Up, Down, Time / Move, and More, with accessible labels and tooltips.
- The More button opens a proper, separate popup for additional sessions, removing one from a block, and choosing background colors.
- Remove routine action confirmations that remained at the bottom of the screen; retain temporary error and safety messages.
- Give Night a muted slate-gray treatment to distinguish the end of the day while respecting the app theme.
- Preserve individual session colors, Unassigned on the left, mobile scrolling and all planning behavior.

## 0.1.1-beta.1 — Clearer sessions and personal colors

- Simplified session cards: leave the main task/habit action and Time / Move visible, put additional controls under **More**.
- Give sessions soft background colors for clearer boundaries. Choose from eight colors, use a custom color picker, or revert to automatic.
- Save colors per individual planned session, including after moving or reopening it. Linked sessions may have separate colors.
- Keep Unassigned on the left to preserve convenient dragging.
- Preserve the four-column timeline and all existing task, habit, mobile and planning behavior.

## 0.1.0 — First preview

Welcome to **Day Blocks**! Plan today's tasks and habits in a continuous, four-part timeline.

- Four blocks: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Night, in a single horizontal row.
- Cleaner session actions: primary controls stay visible, while secondary actions appear in a tidy More menu.
- Colorful sessions: different soft backgrounds by default, with individual preset and custom-color options that are saved with the plan.
- Unassigned stays on the left for straightforward drag-and-drop.
- Drag or use Plan to move tasks and habits out of Unassigned.
- Arrange session order and choose exact times or flexible durations.
- Continue one task or habit across multiple blocks, without duplicating it.
- Reuse preferred times for habits on future scheduled days.
- Update original tasks and habit progress, while keeping planned time separate from completed time.
- Mobile-friendly sideways scrolling, keyboard-accessible actions, and an appearance that respects Super Productivity's fonts and wallpaper.

This is a **preview for user testing**, not an accepted stable release.
